Warning Signs

Common Red Flags

If you see any of these, pause — and never send money or sensitive data.

Payment for “Equipment” or “Training”

You're sent a fake check to buy equipment from a specific vendor, or asked to pay an upfront onboarding fee.

No Live or Technical Interview

An instant offer after 3–4 simple questions in a Google Form or Telegram chat, with no video or voice call.

Mismatched Corporate Domains

A “Fortune 500” recruiter writing from @gmail.com or a misspelled domain like @micros0ft-careers.com.

Verification Guide

Job Verification Checklist

Check the exact domain after the '@'. Real recruiters write from official company domains (e.g. recruiter@google.com). Watch for free public domains or subtle typos.
Go to the company's careers page yourself and search for the job ID or title. If it isn't listed, contact HR through their verified email.
No genuine company asks students for application, ID badge or software fees. Any upfront payment demand is a scam.
